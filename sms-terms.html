SMS Terms

Last updated: October 19, 2026

These SMS Terms apply to the CampusHealthCoach text message program. By checking the consent box at sign-up and providing your mobile number, you agree to these terms and our Privacy Policy.

1. Program name

CampusHealthCoach

2. Program description

CampusHealthCoach is a beta AI health coaching program for college students, delivered by text message. After you sign up, you will receive recurring automated text messages, including:

  • Daily check-in texts asking how your goals and the 5 Pillars of College Health (Hydration, Food, Exercise, Sleep, and Low-stress Reset) went.
  • Coaching replies responding to what you share, with small, practical habit tips.
  • Weekly summaries of your progress, plus optional team updates if you join a team.

3. Message frequency

Message frequency varies, usually about 1–3 messages per day, plus replies to messages you send us.

4. Cost

Message and data rates may apply. Check with your mobile carrier for details about your plan. Any CampusHealthCoach subscription fee is separate and is described on our pricing section.

5. How to opt out

You can cancel text messages at any time. Reply STOP to any message. You will receive one final message confirming that you have been unsubscribed, and you will receive no further messages unless you sign up again. You can also reply STOPALL, UNSUBSCRIBE, CANCEL, END, or QUIT. To rejoin, reply START.

6. How to get help

Reply HELP to any message for help, or email j.jeffmorton@gmail.com.

7. Consent

By opting in, you agree to receive recurring automated coaching texts from CampusHealthCoach at the number you provided. Consent is not a condition of any purchase. You confirm that you are the account holder or authorized user of the mobile number and that you are 18 or older.

8. Carrier disclaimer

Mobile carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. Message delivery is subject to effective transmission from your mobile carrier and is not guaranteed.

9. Your coach is AI

Your CampusHealthCoach coach (Coach Marcus, Coach Deuce, Coach Anna, or Coach Sophia) is an automated AI assistant, not a human. Replies are generated by an AI model and may sometimes be inaccurate or incomplete. Use your own judgment.

10. Not medical advice

CampusHealthCoach provides general wellness and habit coaching only. It is not medical, nutritional, or mental health advice, diagnosis, or treatment, and it does not replace a doctor or other qualified professional. Do not use CampusHealthCoach for urgent needs. Texts are not monitored in real time.

In an emergency, call 911. If you are in crisis or thinking about harming yourself, call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, U.S.) any time.

11. Privacy

We respect your privacy. No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. See our Privacy Policy for full details on how we collect, use, and protect your information.

12. Changes

We may update these SMS Terms from time to time. We will update the date above, and for important changes we will let active users know by text or email.

13. Contact
