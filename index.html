How it works

Coaching that fits between classes

Four simple steps. All through SMS — the app you already open every day.

1

Text in

Opt in with your number. Your AI coach texts you — no downloads, no passwords to forget mid-semester.

2

Daily check-ins

Quick prompts on hydration, food, movement, sleep, and stress. Reply in a sentence — or a word.

3

Accountability teams

Join a small squad of classmates. Light peer nudges keep you honest without the group-chat chaos.

4

Weekly summaries

Every week, a clear recap of wins, patterns, and one focus for next week — so progress feels real.

Five pillars

Health that matches campus life

Small, doable habits across the five areas that matter most when you’re juggling classes, work, and everything else.

Hydration

Gentle reminders to drink water between lectures — before the afternoon crash hits.

Food

Practical prompts for dining halls and late-night study snacks — balance without the guilt spiral.

Exercise

Micro-movement that fits a packed schedule: walks between buildings, short gym sessions, stretch breaks.

Sleep

Wind-down cues and sleep check-ins that respect all-nighters — then help you recover after.

Low-stress Reset

Simple micro-resets for exam weeks and tough days — one breath, one walk, one clear next step.
