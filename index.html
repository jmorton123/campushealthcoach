How it works

Coaching that fits between classes

You drive it. Your coach supports. Small habits you stack, not overhauls. All through SMS, the app you already open every day.

1

Set your goals

You decide what success looks like across the five pillars. Your coach works from your goals, not a one-size plan.

2

Daily check-ins

Text how each pillar went in your own words. No forms, no app. Just a message. Your coach replies to what you share.

3

Accountability teams

Join a small squad of classmates. Light peer nudges keep you honest without the group-chat chaos.

4

Weekly summaries

Every week, a clear recap of wins and patterns toward the goals you set, plus one focus for next week.

Example check-in: “Had a great workout, hydration was solid, and ate well. Sleep and stress relief need some work.”

Framework

The 5 Pillars of College Health

You choose which pillars to focus on and what success looks like. Your coach helps with small habits you stack onto routines you already have, not overhauls.

Hydration

Gentle reminders to drink water between lectures — before the afternoon crash hits.

Food

Real food, fewer wheat and refined carbs. Dining-hall and order-out picks: bowls, protein plates, and easy swaps that skip the bread and fries.

Exercise

Micro-movement that fits a packed schedule: walks between buildings, short gym sessions, stretch breaks.

Sleep

Wind-down cues and sleep check-ins that respect all-nighters — then help you recover after.

Low-stress Reset

Quick breathwork and 2-minute micro-resets you can do between classes. Box breathing, longer exhales, then back to it.
